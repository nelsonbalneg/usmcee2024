# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. What should I do if I cannot log in and receive the message “Log in failed. Check your credentials”?
- Double-check that you are using the correct **registered email and password**.
- Make sure there are no extra spaces or incorrect capitalization.
- Try resetting your password using the **“Forgot Password”** option.
- If the issue persists, contact the **Admissions Office or system support** for assistance.

---

### 2. For retakers, when will the results be released, and how can they enroll if they pass?
- Results for retakers will be released **based on the official announcement of the university**.
- If you pass, you will receive instructions through the **portal or official communication channels**.
- Follow the given steps for **program confirmation and submission of requirements** within the specified deadline.

---

### 3. For freshmen, is enrollment done on campus or online?
- Enrollment procedures may be conducted **online, on campus, or through a hybrid setup**, depending on university guidelines.
- Always check the **official announcements** for the exact process and schedule.

---

### 4. Will there be a second batch of USMCEE?
- Yes, a second batch may be conducted **depending on slot availability and university approval**.
- Stay updated through official **USM announcements and admission portals**.

---

### 5. Is it possible to log in using a different Gmail account?
- No, you must log in using the **Gmail account you used during registration**.
- Using a different account may prevent access to your application and results.

---

### 6. How will I know if I passed?
- You can check your result by logging into the **USMCEE portal**.
- Qualified applicants will see their **admission status and program qualification**.
- Additional instructions will also be provided for the next steps (e.g., confirmation and enrollment).
